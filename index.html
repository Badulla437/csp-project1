About Our Project

Android smartphones have become an important part of everyday life. People use them for communication, education, entertainment, shopping and many other activities.

Our Community Service Project studies how different people use Android phones in their daily lives. We interact with people, collect information through surveys and understand their smartphone usage habits.

Project Objectives

  • To understand Android phone usage among different people.
  • To study how smartphones are used for education.
  • To understand the use of smartphones for communication.
  • To study the use of mobile applications.
  • To understand people's awareness of online safety.
  • To identify the benefits and challenges of smartphone usage.
  • To create awareness about responsible smartphone usage.

Our Project Activities

1. Survey

We collect information from different people about their Android phone usage.

2. Interaction

We interact with people and understand their smartphone habits.

3. Data Collection

We record the information collected during our survey.

4. Data Analysis

We study the collected information and identify common usage patterns.

5. Awareness

We share information about safe and responsible smartphone usage.
