About Our CSP Project

Project Title: A Study on Android Phone Usage Among Different People

College: SREE VENKATESWAEA COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING

Project Type: Community Service Project

Project Description: This project focuses on studying how different people use Android smartphones in their daily lives. We explore their usage patterns, purposes, advantages, disadvantages, and awareness.

Project Objectives

  • To understand Android phone usage among different people.
  • To identify the main purposes of smartphone usage.
  • To study the use of smartphones for education and communication.
  • To understand the benefits and challenges of Android phones.
  • To create awareness about safe and responsible smartphone usage.

Our Project Activities

1. Survey Collection

Collect information from different people about their Android phone usage.

2. Data Analysis

Organize and analyze the collected survey information to understand usage patterns.

3. Awareness

Explain the importance of responsible smartphone usage and digital awareness.

4. Project Documentation

Prepare project findings, photographs, survey results, and conclusions.
